Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the app processes information when installed and used by a merchant on Shopify. It is intended to be factual and transparent. It is not legal advice.

What data the app processes

Merchant and store data

The app processes store data required to run discount campaigns, such as products, collections, and tags selected by the merchant when configuring campaigns in the app admin.

Customer-related data used for discount eligibility

If a merchant configures a campaign to apply only to customers with specific customer tags, the app evaluates whether the logged-in customer has any of those tags at cart or checkout time. The app does not request or process customer identifiers such as name, email, phone number, or address for this purpose.

How the app uses data

The app uses the processed data only to: (1) allow merchants to configure discount campaign rules, (2) determine eligibility and apply automatic discounts in cart and checkout, and (3) support an Easy Order flow by applying a configurable rule set to eligible products via an automatic discount.

Where configuration is stored

The app stores campaign configuration in shop metafields and creates/updates them automatically when merchants save changes in the app. The app may also store technical identifiers required to operate correctly (for example, cached IDs for the configured discount or function).

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell personal data.
  • It does not use personal data for marketing or advertising.
  • It does not store customer identifiers such as name, email, phone, or address in the app database.

Data retention

Customer eligibility checks (such as customer tag membership) are evaluated at runtime to apply discounts and are not persisted by the app. Merchant configuration data (campaign rules) is retained while the app is installed, and can be removed by uninstalling the app and/or deleting app-related metafields from the store.

Security

The app uses HTTPS/TLS for data in transit when communicating with Shopify. The app follows a principle of data minimization and avoids logging customer personal data.

Third parties

The app does not share customer personal data with third parties. Shopify is the platform provider that supplies data to the app under the merchant’s authorization.
